About

I own the analytics layer of McKesson’s Finance Data Product. Data engineering lands SAP data in Databricks; I take it from there. I validate and reconcile it, model it into a certified subject-area semantic model, secure it with row-level security, and deliver it to Finance teams, other business units, and AI agents.

I also lead my team’s move to agentic, code-based BI. With Claude Code, semantic models, reports, and documentation are built as code under GitHub version control, guided by reusable skills that encode our modeling standards. My background in accounting, banking, and portfolio management means I care about what the numbers mean, not only how they are built.
